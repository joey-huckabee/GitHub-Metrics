# GitHub-Metrics — Level 3 Requirements

## Purpose

This document establishes the Level 3 (L3) requirements for GitHub-Metrics. L3
requirements are implementation-level derivations of the L2 requirements in
[`L2.md`](L2.md): they name the concrete behaviour a maintainer can change and
break, and they are the level at which tests attach.

## Conventions

Each L3 is a single line, because at implementation level the obligation is
almost always one sentence and a full section per item buries it:

```
**L3-<CAT>-<NNN>** · Parent: L2-<CAT>-<NNN> · Verification: <methods>
<statement>
```

An optional `· Evidence: ...` follows the verification methods, and is required
before a non-Test method counts as satisfied.

Verification methods use the DO-178 letters defined in [`L1.md`](L1.md): T, I,
A, D.

**Status is not recorded here.** It is derived in
[`TRACE-MATRIX.md`](TRACE-MATRIX.md) by `scripts/build-trace-matrix.py` from
the `@pytest.mark.requirement` markers in `tests/`. A test claims an L3 by
naming it:

```python
@pytest.mark.requirement("L3-ING-002")
def test_a_byte_order_mark_does_not_hide_the_header() -> None:
    ...
```

## L3-ING: Repository inventory ingestion

**L3-ING-001** · Parent: L2-ING-001 · Verification: T
`read_repository_csv` shall return a `RepositoryRef` per accepted row exposing
`owner`, `repoid`, `full_name` as `owner/repoid`, `url` as
`https://github.com/owner/repoid`, and `source_line` set to the row's 1-based
physical line.

**L3-ING-002** · Parent: L2-ING-002 · Verification: T
The reader shall decode input as `utf-8-sig`, so that a leading byte-order mark
is consumed rather than becoming part of the first header cell.

**L3-ING-003** · Parent: L2-ING-003 · Verification: T
The reader shall hand newline handling to the `csv` module by opening its text
with `newline=""`, so that CRLF and LF inputs parse identically and a newline
inside a quoted field does not split the row.

**L3-ING-004** · Parent: L2-ING-004 · Verification: T
Header cells shall be compared after stripping whitespace and case-folding, the
`owner` and `repoid` columns shall be located by name rather than by position,
and any other column shall be ignored.

**L3-ING-005** · Parent: L2-ING-005 · Verification: T
A row whose every cell is empty after stripping shall be skipped without being
counted or reported, and the `owner` and `repoid` values of an accepted row
shall be stripped of surrounding whitespace.

**L3-ING-006** · Parent: L2-ING-006 · Verification: T
`RepositoryRef.key` shall case-fold both components, and the reader shall use it
to drop a repeated repository, reporting the line of the first occurrence.

**L3-ING-007** · Parent: L2-ING-007 · Verification: T
A quoted field containing a separator shall be read as one cell, and an
inventory of 5,000 rows shall be read by the ordinary code path.

## L3-VAL: Name validation

**L3-VAL-001** · Parent: L2-VAL-001 · Verification: T
`validate_owner` shall accept a name of 1 to 39 characters drawn from letters,
digits and hyphens, with no leading hyphen, no trailing hyphen and no
consecutive hyphens, and shall reject anything else.

**L3-VAL-002** · Parent: L2-VAL-002 · Verification: T
`validate_repoid` shall accept a name of 1 to 100 characters drawn from letters,
digits, hyphens, underscores and dots, excluding `.`, `..` and any name ending
in `.git`.

**L3-VAL-003** · Parent: L2-VAL-003 · Verification: T
Both validators shall return `None` for an accepted name and a phrase naming the
first problem for a rejected one.

**L3-VAL-004** · Parent: L2-VAL-002 · Verification: T
The `.git` rejection shall match the suffix case-insensitively and shall not
reject a name that merely contains `git` or `.git` elsewhere.

## L3-ERR: Error reporting and taxonomy

**L3-ERR-001** · Parent: L2-ERR-001 · Verification: T
A missing path shall raise `SourceNotFoundError`, an unopenable path
`SourceUnreadableError`, a file with no header `SourceEmptyError`, a header
lacking or duplicating a required column `HeaderError`, non-UTF-8 bytes
`SourceDecodeError`, and content that is binary or unparseable
`MalformedCsvError` — each rendering its code.

**L3-ERR-002** · Parent: L2-ERR-002 · Verification: T
A rejected row shall append a `RowIssue` carrying its code, physical line and a
message naming the offending value, rendered as `path:line: [CODE] message`.

**L3-ERR-003** · Parent: L2-ERR-003 · Verification: T
With `strict=True` the first `RowIssue` shall instead raise `StrictModeError`
naming that issue, and a file with no issues shall produce the same result under
both modes.

**L3-ERR-004** · Parent: L2-ERR-004 · Verification: T
Every ingestion error shall derive from `IngestError`, and the codes of the
error classes shall be pairwise distinct.

## L3-CON: Concurrency

**L3-CON-001** · Parent: L2-CON-001 · Verification: T
`read_repository_csvs` shall read its sources on a `ThreadPoolExecutor`, shall
return one result per source, shall start no worker for an empty input, and
shall propagate strict-mode failures out of the pool.

**L3-CON-002** · Parent: L2-CON-002 · Verification: T
Results shall be ordered by input position, repeated runs over the same sources
shall be identical, and when two sources fail the error raised shall be the one
belonging to the earlier source.

**L3-CON-003** · Parent: L2-CON-003 · Verification: T
Reads performed with 1, 2 and 16 workers shall produce identical results, and a
worker count exceeding the source count shall be harmless.

## L3-CLI: CLI capability surface

**L3-CLI-001** · Parent: L2-CLI-001 · Verification: T
`github-metrics ingest` shall accept one or more paths, shall require at least
one, shall appear in the command list, and shall summarise a multi-file run with
a combined total.

**L3-CLI-002** · Parent: L2-CLI-002 · Verification: T
`ingest` shall succeed with no `GITHUB_TOKEN` in the environment, in the same
conditions under which `rate-limit` refuses to run.

**L3-CLI-003** · Parent: L2-CLI-003 · Verification: T
`--format json` shall write parseable JSON to standard output, and `--output`
shall write the report to the named file instead.

**L3-CLI-004** · Parent: L2-CLI-004 · Verification: T
`ingest` shall exit 0 for a clean read, 3 when rows were rejected, and 2 when a
source could not be read or a strict-mode read was abandoned.

## L3-LOG: Diagnostic logging

**L3-LOG-001** · Parent: L2-LOG-001 · Verification: T
Each `RowIssue` shall be logged at DEBUG on the `github_metrics.ingest` logger,
and each completed file shall be logged at INFO with its accepted, read and
rejected counts.

## L3-OUT: Result output and destinations

**L3-OUT-001** · Parent: L2-OUT-001 · Verification: T
`SoftwareRow.to_header()` shall derive the nineteen column names and their
order from the dataclass field order, matching the header documented in
`METRICS.md`, and the documented reference row shall render exactly.

**L3-OUT-002** · Parent: L2-OUT-002 · Verification: T
`write_csv` shall emit the header for an empty sequence, preserve the given row
order, and terminate lines with LF on every platform.

**L3-OUT-003** · Parent: L2-OUT-003 · Verification: T
`write_json` shall emit an array of objects whose keys are the selected column
names, keep integers, floats and booleans as JSON types, render `datetime` and
`UUID` as the same strings the CSV uses, and repeat the run identity per
object.

**L3-OUT-004** · Parent: L2-OUT-007 · Verification: T
A row with no measurements shall keep its identity columns and render every
metric and score as an empty CSV field and as JSON `null`, while a row measured
as zero shall render `0`.

**L3-OUT-005** · Parent: L2-OUT-007 · Verification: T
Booleans shall render as `true` and `false`, and floats shall render at full
repr precision.

**L3-OUT-006** · Parent: L2-OUT-005 · Verification: T
`resolve_fields` shall return every column for `None`, an empty selection or a
selection of blanks; shall return canonical order regardless of the order
given; and shall accept mixed case, padding and duplicates.

**L3-OUT-007** · Parent: L2-OUT-006 · Verification: T
`resolve_fields` shall raise `UnknownFieldError` carrying `GM-OUT-001` for an
unrecognised name, naming the closest valid match when one exists.

**L3-OUT-008** · Parent: L2-OUT-004 · Verification: T
`render_console` shall emit one aligned label-and-value line per selected
column, separate consecutive repositories with a rule, mark an uncollected
value rather than leaving the line blank, and state plainly when there are no
rows.

**L3-OUT-009** · Parent: L2-OUT-008 · Verification: T
`resolve_destination` shall return `None` for no destination, append
`githubmetrics.csv` or `githubmetrics.json` inside a directory or a
separator-terminated path, use a named file as given, and raise
`OutputDestinationError` carrying `GM-OUT-002` when the parent directory is
absent.

**L3-OUT-010** · Parent: L2-OUT-009 · Verification: T
`ScanIdentifier` shall default to a fresh UUID4 and a timezone-aware UTC
timestamp, shall reject a naive timestamp, and one instance shall stamp every
row of a run.
